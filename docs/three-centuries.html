Russia · China · Europe

Three powers, three centuries

For each decade, the bar is the share of years its states spent at war: Russia's successive states (Tsardom to Federation), China's (Qing to People's Republic), and all European states together. Lines on top count the conflicts they entered. Russia's states were at war in — of all years since 1700, China's in —; Europe's share fell from — in the 1700s to — after 1945.

Wikidata records who took part in a war, not who started it, so this cannot separate wars of expansion from wars of defence. A state's lineage is its Correlates of War code in the HistWarDB crosswalk; "Europe" is COW codes 200–395 without Russia. Very long items (the Sino-Japanese Wars of 1894–1945, the Kashmir conflict) inflate the share for the states in them.
